# E-Commerce Refund Policy

## General Rules

1. **Delivery requirement**: The customer must have `order_status = "delivered"` to qualify for any refund request.

2. **Time window**: Refunds are allowed within **30 days of delivery** only, unless an exception below applies.

3. **One refund per customer per 30-day window**: Each customer may receive at most **one approved refund** within any rolling 30-day period. Repeat refund requests within this window are flagged as potential abuse and must be denied unless reviewed by a supervisor.

## Refund Categories

### Change of Mind
- Allowed only if the item is **unused and unopened**.
- A **15% restocking fee** applies to the refund amount.
- Must be requested within 30 days of delivery.

### Defective or Damaged Items
- **Full refund** with **free return shipping**.
- No time limit up to **90 days** from delivery.
- Requires confirmation that the item is defective or damaged.

### Final Sale Items
- Items marked **"Final Sale"** are **non-refundable**, except when the item is **defective** (see above).

### Digital Goods
- Digital goods are **non-refundable once downloaded**.
- If the digital good has not been downloaded, a refund may be considered within 30 days of purchase.

## Denial Criteria

Refunds must be **denied** when:
- Order status is not `"delivered"`.
- Request is outside the applicable time window.
- Item is Final Sale and not defective.
- Digital good has already been downloaded.
- Customer has already received an approved refund in the last 30 days.
- Item shows signs of use (for change-of-mind requests).

## Approval Process

Agents must verify eligibility using system tools before approving or denying any refund request. Never approve or deny without checking order details, refund history, and policy rules.
